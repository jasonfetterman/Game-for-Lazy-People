# THE GAME FOR LAZY PEOPLE

### Doing Nothing Has Never Been This Much Work.

**A 3D comedy idle-tycoon and life simulation about spending an unreasonable amount of money to avoid doing absolutely anything.**

Build your lifestyle. Buy convenience. Automate everything. Expand your empire. Eventually, become so incredibly lazy that even managing your laziness becomes someone else's responsibility.

---

## 🎮 About the Game

*The Game for Lazy People* is a single-player 3D comedy life simulation and tycoon game built around one central idea:

**What if you could spend your way out of practically every inconvenience in life?**

You begin in a modest apartment, handling everyday responsibilities yourself. Cleaning, cooking, shopping, maintenance, errands, and other mundane activities consume your time and energy.

But why do everything yourself?

Purchase appliances. Install automation systems. Hire services. Deploy robots. Upgrade your property. Build businesses. Develop increasingly ridiculous solutions to ordinary problems.

Your ultimate objective is to create a lifestyle where nearly everything operates automatically.

The catch?

Getting there takes money, planning, upgrades, and an extraordinary amount of effort.

## 💡 Core Gameplay

The game revolves around a progression loop:

1. **Discover inconveniences** — Everyday problems create opportunities.
2. **Earn money** — Complete tasks, gigs, contracts, and business activities.
3. **Purchase solutions** — Acquire appliances, gadgets, robots, and services.
4. **Install automation** — Configure systems to handle repetitive responsibilities.
5. **Expand your lifestyle** — Upgrade apartments, acquire properties, and develop businesses.
6. **Eliminate effort** — Replace manual activities with increasingly sophisticated automation.
7. **Build your empire** — Turn a modest living space into an absurdly automated lifestyle.

## 🎯 Development Philosophy

The game should never make managing convenience more inconvenient than the problems being solved.

Every system should contribute to at least one of the following:

- Save the player time.
- Reduce repetitive effort.
- Create an entertaining decision.
- Provide meaningful progression.
- Generate an amusing situation.
- Make the player's lifestyle visibly better.

The goal is not to create an endless collection of chores.

**The goal is to create a game where the player gets progressively better at avoiding them.**
